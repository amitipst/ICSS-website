A Catalyst for Change

A Catalyst for Positive Change

Every great institution thrives on the strength of its community, and the Indradhanush Chitrakoot Student Society (ICSS) has been established to be that transformative force for IPSTians. (IPST is now known as the Faculty of Engineering and Technology - F.E.T, under MGCGV, Chitrakoot, Satna, MP).

Born from a collective vision shared by students, juniors, seniors, and colleagues alike, ICSS represents more than just an organisation—it embodies our commitment to mutual support and collective growth.

As a registered legal entity, ICSS operates with complete transparency and accountability to its members. We recognised a crucial gap: the need for an officially recognised platform that could genuinely serve our community's diverse needs.

The Land of Inspiration

Rooted in Chitrakoot: The Land of Tapasya

Lord Rama's Tapasya: Our Guiding Spirit

Chitrakoot is a place steeped in sacred history, famously known as the location where **Lord Rama, Mata Sita, and Lakshmana** spent 11 years of their 14-year exile. This history teaches us the principle of **'Tapasya'**—doing great work with limited resources, self-reliance, and unwavering dedication to a higher purpose.

This spirit of disciplined effort and empowerment, even when faced with constraints, is the **core inspiration** for the Indradhanush Chitrakoot Student Society's mission to serve its community.

The Vision of Gramodaya Vishwavidyalaya

The ICSS operates within the academic ecosystem of the **Mahatma Gandhi Chitrakoot Gramodaya Vishwavidyalaya (MGCGV)**, home to the **Faculty of Engineering and Technology (F.E.T), formerly known as IPST.**

This university, founded on the principles of rural upliftment and holistic development, shapes the environment we work in. Our dedication to **Health, Education, and Social Empowerment** is a direct reflection of MGCGV's vision.

Our work focuses on three fundamental pillars:

Health

Improving the livelihoods of our fellow IPST members, empowering them to lead better, more fulfilling lives.

Social Empowerment

Building a sustainable support system that will benefit generations of IPSTians to come.

Education

Addressing immediate needs and fostering a robust societal framework for growth.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To create a thriving community that empowers IPST students and alumni through comprehensive health initiatives, quality education, and meaningful social upliftment, fostering a lasting legacy of care, collaboration, and collective success for all members.

Our Mission

  • Prioritise health and well-being through targeted programmes and financial assistance.
  • Enable continuous learning and skill-building for academic and professional success.
  • Strengthen community ties, promoting mutual support, respect, and shared goals.

These guiding principles shape every decision we make and every programme we launch. We believe that when health, education, and community support work in harmony, extraordinary things become possible.

Our Primary Commitment

Health First

At ICSS, we firmly believe that health is the foundation upon which all success is built. Without physical, mental, and emotional well-being, even the most talented individuals struggle to reach their full potential.

Whether you're facing a medical emergency, experiencing psychological challenges, or need social support during difficult times, ICSS stands ready to help. Our financial assistance programme ensures that no member faces health crises alone.

Emergency Support Available 24/7

No IPST member should ever feel alone during a health crisis. Our dedicated support team is always ready to assist.
